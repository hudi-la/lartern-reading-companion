# Reading Companion

**A conversational reading companion that can see the page you are reading and talk it through with you.**

### [Open Reading Companion →](https://reading-companion.onrender.com/)

Created by [Hu Di](https://hudiworks.com/).

## Stay with the book

Point a phone or computer camera at a printed page, then ask questions aloud as you read. Reading Companion combines live visual context with voice conversation to support close reading, unfamiliar language, and ideas that invite further discussion.

The physical book stays at the center. Ask a follow-up question, interrupt an explanation, or continue reading at your own pace. Companion and explanation modes offer different levels of assistance.

## What it offers

- **Page-aware conversation:** discuss the text visible to the camera.
- **Live voice interaction:** ask questions and interrupt spoken responses.
- **Close-reading explanations:** work through vocabulary, sentences, and meaning.
- **A personal bookshelf:** organize books and return to reading across sessions.
- **Captions and typed questions:** follow the conversation in text and ask without speaking.
