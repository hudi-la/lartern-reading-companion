How to use
  1. Point the camera at the page you're reading.
  2. Prop your phone up so the camera keeps the open book in view.
  3. Ask out loud anytime — a word, a sentence, or background — like a real teacher reading alongside you.
  4. Companion: you read quietly; it answers only when you ask, briefly.
    Close reading: it takes the lead and walks you sentence by sentence — you read and paraphrase, it corrects and clarifies.

How to use

  • 📱
    Prop your phone up — use a stand or lean it on something, so your hands are free to turn pages.
  • Tap the center orb to wake it; tap again to stop.
  • 🗣️
    Just speak to ask; you can interrupt it anytime while it's talking.
  • 🔄
    Two modes: Companion (answers only when you ask) / Close reading (guides you line by line).
  • ⏸
    Pause turns off the camera and mic to save power and cost; resume right where you left off.
